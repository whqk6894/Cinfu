## Cinfu Introduction

Cinfu is a web hosting provider that's been around since 2011, offering services that won't empty your wallet. The company, operated by Closco Ltd., specializes in providing affordable hosting solutions including shared web hosting, VPS servers, dedicated servers, domain registration, and SSL certificates.

What makes Cinfu stand out is their approach to pricing. They focus on delivering reliable service at competitive rates, with servers located across multiple continents including Germany, France, Bulgaria, Netherlands, Sweden, United Kingdom, Moldova, and the United States. The company accepts over 50 payment methods, including cryptocurrencies, which is handy if you prefer more flexible payment options.

Cinfu targets users who know their way around servers or are willing to learn. Their VPS and dedicated server offerings are unmanaged, meaning you get full root access and control, but you're responsible for server configuration and maintenance. This keeps costs down and gives experienced users the freedom they want. For those who prefer a more hands-off approach, their shared hosting plans come with either DirectAdmin or cPanel control panels.

The company has built a reputation over 14+ years of operation, emphasizing features like free DDoS protection, 99.9% uptime guarantee, unlimited bandwidth on most plans, and free SSL certificates. They also provide 24/7 support through email and ticketing systems, though response times can vary from 1 to 4 hours based on user reports.

## Cinfu Reviews

Cinfu has received mixed feedback from users over its 14+ years of operation. Here's what customers are saying:

**Positive Feedback:**

Many long-term users praise Cinfu for reliability and value. One customer mentioned using their services for five years, noting that while you need technical knowledge to configure your own server, the pricing is excellent for what you get. Another user highlighted having a VPS in Europe for over a year with no remembered downtime, appreciating the generous disk space, unlimited traffic, and multiple location options.

Support responsiveness gets particular mention from satisfied customers. One user who contacted support more than 15 times over two years described the team as patient and very helpful, recommending them as the best among providers they've tried. Another four-year customer emphasized satisfaction with the service quality and reasonable pricing, noting support response times between 1-4 hours.

The rock-solid uptime and generous storage allocations are frequently mentioned benefits, with customers appreciating that they can use their VPS for backups due to the large disk space and unlimited bandwidth.

**Critical Feedback:**

Some users have experienced frustrations, particularly regarding customer service communication and technical issues. A few customers felt that support responses lacked helpful detail, describing answers as repetitive or unclear when troubleshooting problems. Response speed was praised, but the actual helpfulness of responses varied.

Technical issues have been reported, including DDoS attacks affecting certain subnets, particularly on France servers. While DDoS protection is advertised, some users felt the protection wasn't as robust as expected during actual attacks.

**Overall Assessment:**

Cinfu appears best suited for users who have technical knowledge and can handle server configuration themselves. The company delivers on its core promise of affordable, reliable hosting with good uptime. However, those who need hand-holding or extensive technical support might find the experience challenging. The service works well for experienced users who know what they're doing and value price-to-performance ratio over premium support.
